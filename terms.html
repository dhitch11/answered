The terms

The whole deal,
in plain words.

This page is short on purpose. Terms pages are usually long because they are hiding something. Ours lists every event that can ever put a number on your bill, defines every word that matters, and tells you how to leave. If your bill and this page ever disagree, this page wins.

Last reviewed 2026-08-14. When any of this changes, this date changes with it.

The one rule

Money only moves when something good happened.

There is no subscription. There is no per minute price. There is no per call price. The base price of having Answered on your line is $0, and it stays $0 no matter how many calls come in. You pay for outcomes, and the full list of them is below.

The definition everything hangs on

What a booked job is.

A job is booked when it has a name, an address, a callback number and a confirmed window. Anything less is free.

That is the whole definition, and it is strict on purpose. A caller who hangs up is free. A wrong number is free. A sales call is free. A question with no appointment is free. A message taken is free. If any one of those four pieces is missing, the call cost you nothing, no matter how long it ran.

The cap

Your bill stops at $549. The cap is yours to move.

Every account starts with a monthly cap of $549. Once your bookings reach the cap, every booking after it that month is free. You can set the cap higher or lower whenever you want. A new cap takes effect at your next billing cycle, never in the middle of one, so a bill never changes shape while you are inside it. The cap never moves without you.

The quiet line

What a quiet line is, in writing.

A quiet line is a line that has booked fewer than three jobs a month for 90 straight days. Only then does the $39 monthly rate exist, and every dollar of it comes back as credit against your bookings. A line that books jobs never sees it. The quiet rate exists so a line we answer all year for almost nothing has an honest price instead of a hidden one, and it announces itself before it ever starts: you get told in writing before the first $39 is billed.

Hold, defined

A human reached means a live person.

Not a menu. Not a recording. Not a promise of a callback. A live person on the line, handed to you. If we are wrong about that, you do not pay. If nobody ever picks up, the price is $0, no matter how many hours we held. Your first hold is free: one, so you can see a real hold receipt before you ever pay for one.

Recover, defined

What recovered means, exactly.

Recovered means the payment lands within 30 days of our last contact on that invoice, or by a date the payer promised in writing. Money that arrives outside that window is yours alone, and we never charge on it. The fee is a share of dollars that actually land in your account. It is never a fee on calls placed, letters sent, or time spent.

The shareStandard · Answered subscriber
Newer invoices10% · 8%
Most invoices15% · 13%
The oldest invoices20% · 18%

Older money is harder to get back, and the price says so. Answered subscribers pay less on every band because the agent already knows the job it booked. You see which band every invoice sits in before we place the first call, so the fee is never a surprise after the fact. Nothing recovered means nothing owed.

Disputes

One tap. One word. VOID.

Every charge shows you the call it came from. If you think a charge is wrong, tap it or send us the word VOID, and it dies. The default outcome is in your favor: we would rather lose the $19 than have you spend a Tuesday arguing about it. If real, finished jobs get voided over and over, we do not argue either. We end the service, and that is the whole remedy.

We never bill backwards. No retroactive charges, no clawbacks, no catch-up bill for something that used to be free. A change to a price or a cap only ever applies forward, from your next billing cycle.

Leaving

Cancellation is one dial code.

Dial one code from your phone and your line is back exactly the way it was, in about ten seconds. There is no person to talk you out of it, no notice period, no exit fee. Your data leaves with you: every transcript, recording, booking record and ledger entry we hold for you, exported on request. And if you ask, we delete our copies too. The Privacy page says how.

The texting program

What you agree to when you ask us to text you.

These are the terms of the Answered messaging program. They apply the moment you give us your mobile number and ask for something back.

This program is not switched on yet. It is not approved by the carriers, so no message has ever been sent under it and none will be until it clears. Until then, everything described below arrives by email instead, and this paragraph comes down the day that changes.

  • What the program is. Answered sends you what you asked for and nothing else: the transcript of a call you placed, a booking link you requested, a confirmation of a time you chose, or a receipt for an outcome you were billed for.
  • How you join. You either type your number into a form on this site under the consent line printed beside it, or you ask for the text out loud on a call you placed to us. There is no other way in. We never add a number ourselves.
  • How often. One to three messages per thing you ask for. Under five a month for most people. If you ask for nothing, you get nothing.
  • What it costs. Nothing from us. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier.
  • How you leave. Reply STOP to any message and the texting stops permanently. Reply HELP and a person helps you. You can also write to info@reddenda.com.
  • Your number is not a product. We never share or sell your mobile phone number, or the fact that you opted in, with anyone, for any marketing or promotional purpose. The Privacy page states this in full.
  • Carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
