What we build

The phone layer for a business that is too busy to answer it.

A missed call at a small company is usually a lost job. We build four things that sit on the phone, and each one is priced for the outcome it produces rather than the minutes it burns.

  • Answered picks up your line, works out what the caller wants, and books it or hands it to you.
  • Hold waits on hold for you and calls you back when a human picks up.
  • Recover chases invoices that have gone quiet.
  • Parley helps two people settle on a price without either of them having to be the one who blinks.

We are a small, self funded company. There is no call centre and there is no sales team. What the site says the product does is what the product does, and where it does not do something yet, the page says so in the same sentence.
