What we build

Anyone can rent a voice. The intelligence is the company.

The parts that make a phone speak are cheap, and they get cheaper every quarter. Good. That means the contest is fought somewhere else: does the call actually arrive, does the agent behave, did the thing you were billed for really happen, and does all of it clear the strictest rule in the country. That is what we build, and it compounds with every call.

01 / Carrier intelligence

Getting the call to arrive

Every carrier speaks a different dialect of forwarding, and the networks confirm success whether or not they mean it. We detect the carrier, send the right string, then place a real call and watch it land. Verification, not instructions.

02 / Guardrails

Behavior you can bet the business on

It never quotes a price, never invents a number, and hands any caller who asks for a person straight to your cell. Enforced in three independent layers and replayed against fifty hard calls every night.

03 / The outcome ledger

Proof that the job is real

We bill on booked jobs, so a booked job has to mean something exact: a name, an address, a callback number, a confirmed window, and the recording that produced it. That record is the invoice, the audit trail, and the product all at once.
