What we build

Anyone can rent a voice. The intelligence is the company.

The parts that make a phone speak are available to everybody, and they get cheaper every quarter. That is good news, because it means the whole contest is fought somewhere else: on whether the call actually arrives, whether the agent behaves, whether the thing you were billed for really happened, and whether all of it is legal in fifty states. That is what we build, and it compounds with every call.

01 / Carrier intelligence

Getting the call to arrive

Every carrier speaks a different dialect of forwarding, and the networks confirm success whether or not they mean it. We detect the carrier, send the right string, then place a real call and watch it land. Verification, not instructions.

02 / Guardrails

Behaviour you can bet the business on

It never quotes a price, never invents a number, and hands any caller who asks for a person straight to your cell. Enforced in three independent layers and replayed against fifty hard calls every night.

03 / The outcome ledger

Proof that the job is real

We bill on booked jobs, so a booked job has to mean something exact: a name, an address, a callback number, a confirmed window, and the recording that produced it. That record is the invoice, the audit trail, and the product all at once.

The pricing law

We sell outcomes.
We do not sell minutes.

Every voice AI company in this category bills you for talk time or for a seat. That makes their incentive the length of the call. Ours is the result of it.

So the meter has exactly three events on it: a job got booked, a human got reached, a dollar got recovered. If a call produces none of those, it is free. Including the ones that ring at two in the morning.
