The arithmetic nobody does

One missed call is not one missed call.

It is a lead that had already decided to buy, that had already found you, and that is now dialling the next number on the list. Nearly half of them never get a person. That is not a service problem. That is your revenue leaving through a phone.

Put a voice on the line and the leak closes. Not perfectly. Not every caller wants something. But the ones who do get booked, at two in the afternoon and at two in the morning.

The whole system

Five parts, and only one of them is something you do.

Fig 1 · call path

Setup

Nothing gets reprinted. Nothing gets ported. You dial one code.

Your number stays your number

The one on the truck, the van, the yard sign, the Google listing, the magnet on the fridge of every customer you have had for fifteen years. It does not change.

Forwarding, not replacement

One carrier code sends calls here first. Another one sends them back. You can turn it off in the truck in ten seconds if you hate it.

Or catch only what you miss

Forward only the busy line, the dead zone, and the calls that ring out. You keep answering when your hands are free. It catches the rest, which is most of them.

Carrier codes come from Verizon and T-Mobile primary documentation, accessed 2026-08-10. Conditional forwarding behaviour varies by carrier and handset and we are bench testing all three major US carriers before we publish a compatibility claim.

Two in the morning

A burst pipe does not leave a voicemail. It calls the next plumber.

After hours is where the category earns its keep, and it is the shift nobody wants. Water and freezing claims run about 1.61 per 100 insured homes a year at roughly $13,954 of average severity. Somebody is going to answer that call. It costs $49 for it to be you.

Then it goes and gets the rest

The invoice that went quiet on day 31 is not a lost cause. It is an unmade phone call.

A polite, persistent follow up on the invoice that has aged past thirty days. Not a collections agency. You, with an assistant who never forgets and never gets embarrassed.

Contingency

Only on what actually lands

For the ones that have genuinely defaulted, a share of the money that arrives. Nothing arrives, nothing is owed. You pay nothing unless the money lands.

Every call

Written down as it happens

Who promised what, and when. Every promise to pay logged, every call recorded where consent permits. It is your record, and it is the reason the second call works.

Included.
